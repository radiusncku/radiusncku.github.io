Research

研究方向

01

Infrastructure resilience

基礎設施韌性

Modeling how bridges, pipelines, and networks degrade and recover under extreme events.

建立橋梁、管線與網路在極端事件下劣化與復原的模型。

02

Structural health monitoring

結構健康監測

Turning sensor data into early, actionable signals of structural change and risk.

將感測資料轉化為結構變化與風險的早期、可行動訊號。

03

Hazard & climate risk

災害與氣候風險

Quantifying how earthquakes, floods, and a changing climate reshape infrastructure risk.

量化地震、洪水與氣候變遷如何重塑基礎設施的風險。

Chia-Wei (Joe) Hsu

許家維

Principal Investigator · Assistant Professor

主持人 · 助理教授

Joe leads RADIUS in the Department of Civil Engineering at National Cheng Kung University. His group works at the intersection of structural engineering, sensing, and data science to make infrastructure more resilient to natural hazards.

許家維為國立成功大學土木工程學系助理教授，主持 RADIUS 實驗室。研究橫跨結構工程、感測技術與資料科學，致力於提升基礎設施面對天然災害的韌性。
