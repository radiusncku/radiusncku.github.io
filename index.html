About

關於

Where infrastructure meets human behavior

基礎設施與人類行為的交會處

RADIUS works where the built environment meets human behavior. Using urban informatics and data analytics, we study how complex infrastructure systems respond under stress — from cascading technical failures to how people actually behave during a disaster.

RADIUS 實驗室的研究，落在建成環境與人類行為交會的地方。我們運用都市資訊學與數據分析，探討複雜的基礎設施系統在壓力下如何反應——從技術上的連鎖失效，到災害當下人們真實的行為反應。

Bridging data science and civil engineering, we build the computational frameworks for cities that are not just robust, but antifragile. We care about reach: how a single failure propagates through a network, and how a single data-driven intervention can safeguard an entire city.

我們銜接資料科學與土木工程，打造讓城市不只穩健、更能「反脆弱」的計算框架。我們在意的是影響的「半徑」：一個失效如何在網路中擴散，而一次精準、以資料驅動的介入，又能如何守護整座城市。

Research

研究方向

01

Disaster informatics

災難資訊

Turning raw data into actionable intelligence for emergency response and recovery.

將原始資料轉化為緊急應變與復原所需的決策情報。

02

Urban resilience

都市韌性

Analyzing how cities absorb shocks, recover, and where they remain most fragile.

分析城市如何吸收衝擊、復原，以及最脆弱的環節所在。

03

Human dynamics

人類行為動態

Decoding how people move, decide, and adapt as urban risks shift around them.

解析人們在都市風險變動下如何移動、決策與調適。

04

Infrastructure systems

基礎設施系統

Tracing how failures spread through networks — and how to stop them from cascading.

追蹤失效如何在網路中傳遞，以及如何阻止其連鎖擴散。

05

Sustainable development

永續發展

Designing today's solutions so they don't compromise tomorrow's safety and resources.

讓今日的解決方案，不犧牲明日的安全與資源。
