Chia-Wei Hsu

許家維

Principal Investigator · Assistant Professor

主持人 · 助理教授

Chia-Wei Hsu is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Civil Engineering at National Cheng Kung University, where he leads the RADIUS Lab. His research combines civil engineering with urban informatics and data science to study how cities — and the people in them — withstand and recover from disasters, spanning urban resilience, human mobility, disaster informatics, and infrastructure networks. He previously conducted postdoctoral research at Texas A&M University.

許家維為國立成功大學土木工程學系助理教授，主持 RADIUS 實驗室。研究結合土木工程、都市資訊學與資料科學，探討城市以及其中的人們如何承受災害並從中復原，涵蓋都市韌性、人類移動行為、災難資訊與基礎設施網路。先前於美國德州農工大學（Texas A&M University）從事博士後研究。
